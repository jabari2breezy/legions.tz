# Legions — Proof of work refresh

**Stack:** React + Vite + GSAP. The existing lightweight app remains the right foundation; the work is editorial and image-led rather than a 3D product demonstration.

## Concept card

- **Idea:** A field ledger of care — each project becomes a numbered, full-bleed proof rather than another section of copy.
- **Metaphor:** Pages from a community field book: index, image, outcome, then the next entry.
- **Signature:** A desktop scroll rail where the image advances in depth while the project metadata settles alongside it; on small screens, it becomes a tactile vertical sequence without hiding content.
- **Emotion:** Clear, hands-on, optimistic.
- **Motion verb:** *Gather and settle* — photos move with a slow measured parallax, typography resolves into place, numbers arrive last.
- **Screenshot:** A large documentary image framed by its two-digit project index and oversized, optically aligned title.

## System

- **Palette:** deep navy `#061a45`, ink `#102c70`, paper `#f4f7fb`, fog `#dfe9f7`, cyan `#62f0d3`, signal blue `#145cbc`.
- **Type:** DM Sans for functional clarity; Playfair Display italic only for emphasis. Body remains 16–18 px at 1.55 leading.
- **Grid:** 12 columns / 8vw outer gutter on desktop; 24px mobile gutter. Project images occupy seven columns and stories five.
- **Shape:** no decorative card radius; the photography reads as evidence, not as UI chrome.
- **Motion:** premium deceleration (`cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`); 160ms interaction, 650ms reveal, scroll translation capped to preserve legibility.

## Assumptions

Project photography and facts are supplied by Legions. The supplied turquoise mark is used as a subtle navigation/section artifact rather than replacing the existing wordmark.
